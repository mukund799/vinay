About Us

NIRDS empowers organizations with data-driven intelligence. Our team ensures accuracy, integrity, and actionable insights across diverse sectors. We provide strategic guidance, uncover market opportunities, and help clients make profitable decisions with confidence.

Mission and Expertise

North India Research and Development Solutions (NIRDS) is an independent social science research and advisory organization committed to supporting institutional projects, conducting rigorous fieldwork, and implementing qualitative and quantitative research methodologies across northern India.

Geographic Scope

Our focus includes all Indian states and union territories located north of Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, and West Bengal—excluding Jammu and Ladakh. Plans are underway to expand into the North-Eastern region. This wide coverage enables us to respond effectively to diverse socio-economic challenges.

Core Activities

NIRDS specializes in large-scale field surveys, social experimentation, program evaluations, and community engagement initiatives. We apply both quantitative and qualitative data collection and analysis, striving to generate actionable insights that inform inclusive and sustainable development policies.
